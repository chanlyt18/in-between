About this prototype

Connections, made visible.

This standalone study explores a second brain as a living landscape. All 28 notes are fictional examples. The 13,500 small points create the sculpture; they are not memory records.

Search notes, select a marker, or regroup by project and automata. Nothing is connected, saved, or sent to a service.
